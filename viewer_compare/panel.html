What the calculation means

Linear products: Δ = B − A. Aspect: Δ = ((B − A + 180) mod 360) − 180 degrees. Wrapped phase: the same shortest signed difference with π and 2π. Masks: Average compares the original passing fractions. The 0–1 preview applies A's selected cutoff to both original fractions, then subtracts the states (−1 = lost pass, 0 = unchanged, +1 = gained pass). A fraction at or above the cutoff passes; missing blocks stay blank. Change A's mask preview controls in the terrain legend to update both inputs.

Mean = ΣΔ/n; MAE = Σ|Δ|/n; RMSE = √(ΣΔ²/n). These describe disagreement, not accuracy unless A is appropriate ground truth. No vertical-datum conversion is performed. Scientific metadata, timestamps, and acquisition compatibility still need review.
